    Optional 3D workflow. Display GroundSurf evidence alongside mapped geology and seismic profiles. This video demonstrates Current View to ParaView and SeisPlotPy. https://youtu.be/eWNIJKgonms
    Optional geostatistics. Use SGeMS Workbench with prepared well measurements to explore groundwater surfaces with kriging and simulation. https://github.com/wtzet-eng/sgems-workbench
